Colorectal Cancer Case Study

Definition

Malignancy of the colon or rectum epithelium. Mostly Adenocarcinoma, developing slowly from adenomatous polyps.

Common Factors

  • Diet high in red/processed meat, obesity, and smoking.
  • Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD).
  • Genetic syndromes: Lynch Syndrome & FAP.

Pathology Pictures

+ Gross Appearance

Exophytic fungating mass or infiltrating "napkin-ring" lesion causing stricture.

+ Microscopic

Infiltrative irregular glands lined by stratified malignant cells with hyperchromatic nuclei.

Clinical Symptoms

  • Change in bowel habits (diarrhea, constipation, or narrowing of stool).
  • Rectal bleeding or blood in the stool (melena or hematochezia).
  • Persistent abdominal discomfort (cramps, gas, or pain).
  • Unexplained weight loss and iron-deficiency anemia due to chronic blood loss.

Prevention & Screening

  • Colonoscopy: Gold standard for polyp detection and removal.
  • Stool Tests: Annual FIT or gFOBT for occult blood.
  • Tumor Marker: CEA (used for monitoring recurrence, not screening).
